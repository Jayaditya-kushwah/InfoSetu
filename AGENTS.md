# AI Agents Architecture

RTI-Ease utilizes specialized prompt-engineered workflows to act as autonomous civic assistants.

## 1. The Legal Draftsman Agent
* **Role:** Translates unstructured citizen inputs into formal legal prose.
* **System Instructions:** Restructures input text into numbered, objective questions that conform to standard RTI filing requirements while automatically filtering out emotional or irrelevant statements.

## 2. The Department Routing Agent
* **Role:** Categorizes the issue domain to find the correct administrative body.
* **System Instructions:** Analyzes the core entity of the grievance (e.g., roads, municipal budgets, environment) and matches it to the respective local, state, or central ministry/department.
