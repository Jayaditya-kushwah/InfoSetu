# RTI-Ease User Manual

Welcome to RTI-Ease! Follow these steps to generate your official RTI application draft.

## Step-by-Step Guide

### Step 1: Input Your Grievance
* Navigate to the homepage.
* In the main text area, describe your civic issue in plain language (e.g., *"Our neighborhood park has been commercialized without public notice"*).

### Step 2: AI Processing & Review
* Click **"Generate Draft"**.
* The system will automatically format your issue into a formal RTI request, complete with relevant legal clause citations. Review the text on the screen.

### Step 3: Identify the Department
* The application will suggest the specific government department and Public Information Officer (PIO) relevant to your geographic location.

### Step 4: Download PDF
* Click **"Download RTI Document"** to save a beautifully formatted, ready-to-mail PDF file.
